Mirror Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Overview

Mirror is a simple camera mirror app. It shows a live front-facing camera preview and an adjustable screen-edge light to help you see your face more clearly.

Camera Access

Mirror asks for camera access so it can display the live selfie preview. Camera frames are used only on your device to show the mirror view.

Data Collection

Mirror does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information. The app does not upload photos, videos, camera frames, or usage data to any server.

Third Parties

Mirror does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Mirror does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the app changes. Updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the support information listed with the app.
