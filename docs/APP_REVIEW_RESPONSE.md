## Notes / reply text

Just a Mirror! is a simple iPhone mirror for everyday checks such as hair and makeup. It gives users a live mirrored front-camera preview, an optional white screen-edge light, and 1–3× digital zoom. Its value is direct access to these tools without ads, accounts, tracking or purchases.

To test it, launch the app and allow camera access. The mirror opens immediately. Tap the light control to toggle the white border and brightness boost, and move the Zoom slider for a closer view. Turn the light off or leave the app to restore the previous screen brightness. If camera access is denied, tap Open Settings, enable access, and return to the app. No sign-in, sample file, paid upgrade or special setup is needed.

Core functionality uses Apple's SwiftUI, UIKit and AVFoundation frameworks on the device. There are no third-party SDKs, backend services or external accounts. Camera frames are not saved or uploaded. The app does not collect data.

Functionality is consistent in every region where the app is offered; there are no region-specific feature gates or content. The app does not publish user-generated content or a third-party media catalog and does not provide medical, financial, gambling or other regulated services.

The physical-device recording Mirror-iPhone-1.0-17-demo.mp4 shows launch and the typical mirror, light and zoom flow. It was captured on an iPhone13Pro running iOS27.2 build24B5099f, with app version1.0 build17. There is no authentication, purchase or user-content flow to demonstrate.

## Product page description draft

Just a Mirror! turns your iPhone's front camera into a simple mirror.

Check your hair or makeup with a live mirrored preview. Turn on the white screen-edge light when you need a little extra light, and use 1–3× digital zoom for a closer look.

No ads, accounts, tracking or purchases. Camera frames stay on your device and are never saved or uploaded.

Requires camera permission. You can manage access in Settings.
